Product contract

What must remain true

Canonical sourceHomepage and English feature pages originate from current https://chatcut.io production HTML, stored as raw snapshots before modification.
Visual identityProduction DOM, layout, copy, videos, images and Astro product islands are preserved. The patch adds causal interaction, not a redesign.
Prototype boundaryShared DemoSession is local-only. Triggered image/video/music/motion states reveal existing production assets/UI; they are not evidence of a new backend generation request.
Route boundary/ receives the playable patch. Mirrored English /features/* pages remain production mirrors. Other product CTAs continue to the real ChatCut product.
Delivery boundaryHuman-facing changes require human review; preview/build success does not authorize merge.
